## Pumpkin Spice Syrup

- 0.25 cup Water
- 0.5 cup Maple Syrup
- 1 Tbsp Pumpkin Spice
- Boil then cool

## Ingredients

- 2 oz Gold Tequila
- 1 oz Pumpkin Spice Syrup
- 0.5 oz Lemon juice
- 1 oz Pumpkin Puree
- Shake and serve over ice
- Chocolate & Black Sprinkles for Rim
