## Candied Almond Garnish

- 1 cup Almonds
- 0.25 cup white granulated sugar
- 1 Tbsp unsalted butter
- Combine in a small pot
- Cook for 5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 oz Baileys Almande
- 2 oz Amaretto liqueur
- 2 oz Heavy Cream
- Shake with and serve on ice
