It’s good weather to enjoy a hot cocoa together! Awoo! So, let’s sit by the fire and warm up with a “Peppermint Snow Mew,” made for the adorable [@DariusTheSnow](https://twitter.com/DariusTheSnow)! Cheers Awoo! ❤️🐺🍸

## Ingredients

- 0.5 Cup White Chocolate
- 1.5 Cups Milk
- 2 Cups Marshmallows
- 1 oz Baileys
- 1.75 oz Peppermint Schnapps
- Heat milk and marshmallows, stir until smooth
- Pour over chocolate and add liquors
- Mix and top with whip-cream and marshmallows
