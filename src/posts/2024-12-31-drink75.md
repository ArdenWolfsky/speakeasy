## Ingredients

- Orange Peel
- 1 ½ oz Hennessy
- 0.5 oz Amaretto
- 2-3 Dashes Orange Bitters
- Stir with ice
- 1/8 tsp Gold Luster Dust
- Top with a Brut Champagne
