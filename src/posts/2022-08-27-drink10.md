A Ryazon Dergy for [@RyazonBlack](https://twitter.com/RyazonBlack).

## Ingredients

- 0.75 oz Lemon Juice
- 2 oz Rum
- 1 oz Cherry Brandy
- 0.75 oz Grape Schnapps
- Splash of Blue Curaçao
- Shake Over Ice
- 1.5 oz Grenadine
- 151 for Flair (Optional)
