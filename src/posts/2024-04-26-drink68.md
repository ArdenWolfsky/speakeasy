The 1856 New York Sour! A personal favorite: extra sour please, that comes with an exciting legacy that includes Pirates, the Royal Navy, Scurvy, and the title "The Cocktail of America." 🎵 Music by [@LastUrsa](https://twitter.com/LastUrsa)🎤

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoonful of sugar
- 0.25 of a lemon
- Half a wine glass of whiskey
- Stir with ice
- Top with a red wine (dry)
