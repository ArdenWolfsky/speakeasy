## Raspberry Syrup

- 12 oz Raspberries
- 0.5 Cup Sugar
- 2 oz Raspberry Liqueur
- Cook until smooth

## Ingredients

- 2 oz Raspberry Syrup
- 2 oz White Chocolate Liqueur
- 1 oz Vanilla Vodka
- 2 oz Raspberry Liqueur
- 4 oz Half & Half
- Shake over ice
