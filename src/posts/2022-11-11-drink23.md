## Ingredients

- 2 oz Apple Whisky
- 1.5 oz Salted Caramel Baileys
- 0.5 oz Fireball Whisky
- Shake over ice
- 1 Scoop vanilla ice cream
- Pour over ice cream
- Top with Caramel Sauce, Pecan Shortbread Cookies, whip cream, and Caramel Apples
