## Shaker 1

- 4 oz Kinky Blue
- 1 oz Orgeat Syrup
- 0.25 oz Egg White
- 0.5 oz Lemon Juice
- Dry Shake
- Add ice and shake for 10 seconds
- Pour into a tall glass

## Shaker 2

- 0.75 oz Empress Indigo Gin
- 0.25 oz Blue Curaçao
- Shake With ice
- Pour over base mix
