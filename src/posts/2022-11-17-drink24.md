## Ingredients

- 2 Tablespoons Pumpkin Puree
- 2 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Spiced Rum
- 0.5 oz Baileys
- 1 oz Maple Syrup
- 0.25 teaspoon Vanilla Paste
- Shake with ice
- Top with whip cream and a dusting of pumpkin spice
