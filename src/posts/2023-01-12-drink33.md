I present to you “The Blue Bean!” 🐾A fun and tasty mocktail Made for the adorable and wonderful [@That1VRGamer](https://twitter.com/That1VRGamer) ❤️🐺 Cheers Awoo!

## Ingredients

- 1 Cup Sour Patch Kids
- 6 oz Water
- Boil & cool
- 3 oz Ritual Tequila Alternative (can use Regular Tequila instead)
- 2 oz Lemon Juice
- 1.5 oz Sour Patch Syrup
- Shake with Ice
- 1.5 oz Blueberry Simple Syrup
- Garnish with Lemon and Candy
