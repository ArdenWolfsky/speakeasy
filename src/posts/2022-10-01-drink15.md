## Ingredients

- 3 oz Apple Whisky
- 1 oz Vodka
- 0.5 oz Lemon juice
- 1 oz Cherry Brandy
- 1.5 oz Egg whites
- Dry shake 30 seconds
- Add ice
- Shake 30 seconds
- 1 oz Grenadine
- Add dry ice for effect (do not consume or touch with bare skin)
