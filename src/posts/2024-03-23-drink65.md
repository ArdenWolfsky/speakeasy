## Ingredients

- 0.5 a liqueur glass of Vermouth
- 0.5 a wineglassful of Scotch whiskey
- 3 – 4 drops of Angostura Bitters
- Ditto of plain syrup
- Stir with ice
- Strain and top with cocktail cherries or lemon
