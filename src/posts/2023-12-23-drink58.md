## Ingredients

- 2 oz Gin
- 1 oz Vermouth
- 2 oz Simple Syrup
- 2 oz Peppermint Schnapps
- 1.5 oz Snow Globe Liqueur

## Steps

- Add sprinkles to Glass
- Decorecte with Rosemary, Cranberries & Candy
- Add a little water, then freeze
- Mix drink ingredients with ice
- Pour & top with Tonic Water
