## Ingredients

- 0.25 Cup Coarse Coffee
- 6 oz Vodka
- Sleep 48 Hours
- 2 oz Coffee Vodka
- 2 oz Caramel Creamer
- 2 oz Baileys (or any Coffee liqueur)
- Shake with ice
- Caramel & Chocolate Sauce for Garnish
- Top with Whip Cream
