## Ingredients

- Half a Cucumber
- Half a Lime (Juiced)
- Pinch of Salt
- 6 Mint Leaves
- 3 Tbsp Maple Syrup
- 4 oz Ginger Beer
- Top with Ginger Beer

Replace 2 oz of Ginger beer with Gin to make this an alcoholic beverage.
