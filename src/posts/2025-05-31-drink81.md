## Ingredients

- 3 oz Hibiscus Rose Gin
- 2 oz Simple Syrup
- 1 oz Fresh Lime Juice
- 1 oz Crème de Violette
- 1 oz Pomegranate Juice

## Make

- Shake with ice
- Serve with lime peal and edible rose petals
