## Ingredients

- 1 Bottle of Red Wine
- 1 Orange Slice
- 2 Whole Anise Stars
- 6 Whole Cloves
- 6 Cardamom Pods
- 3 Cinnamon Sticks
- 0.25 teaspoon Allspice
- 2 Tablespoons Brown Sugar
- Simmer at least 20 Mins
- Serve warm with a fresh orange slice
