The weekend is here! So, let’s dive into something sweet and fun! What you ask? How about a refreshing glass of “Puzz Punch” Awoo! Made for the one of a kind [@Puzz156](https://twitter.com/Puzz156)! 🍸❤️

## Ingredients

- 2 oz Midori Melon Liqueur
- 2 oz Malibu Rum
- 4 oz Pineapple Juice
- Shake with ice
- Pour and serve with a lime popsicle
