## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp Pumpkin Puree
- 0.25 tsp Pumpkin Spice
- 2 oz Apple Cider
- 4 oz Maple Whisky
- Shake with ice
- Garnish glass with Maple Syrup and Cinnamon Sugar
- Serve over Apple Cider Ice
