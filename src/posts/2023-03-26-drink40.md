When life gets you down, the best fix is to cuddle up and hug “The Bestest Muttz,” [@JamesTheMuttz](https://twitter.com/JamesTheMuttz), to make it all better! ❤️Big wolfy hugz cutie! Love ya! Cheers Awoo! 🍹

## Ingredients

- 1.5 oz Dark Rum
- 2 oz Pineapple Juice
- 1.5 oz Light Rum
- 1 oz Orange Juice
- 0.25 oz Grenadine
- 0.25 oz Lime Juice
- Shake over ice
- Top with Sprite
