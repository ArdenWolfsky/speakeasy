## Ingredients

- Muddle in glass
- 2 oz Mango
- 8 Mint Leaves
- 0.5 tsp Sugar
- Fill with Ice

## Shaker 1

- 1 oz Grand Marnier
- 1 oz Orgeat Syrup
- 4 oz Mango Vodka
- Ice & Shake

## Shaker 2

- 0.5 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Ume Plum Liqueur
- 1 oz Egg whites
- Dry shake
- Pour over Mango Base
