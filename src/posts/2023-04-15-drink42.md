It’s the perfect time for some beach time fun! 🏖️So, grab your Life Jacket and lets catch some waves! Big Wolfy Hugz to the best “Buoyant Shep” [@BetaEtaDelota](https://twitter.com/BetaEtaDelota) Awoo! 🍸❤️

## Shaker 1

- 2 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Kahlua
- 0.5 oz Caramel
- Shake with ice

## Shaker 2

- 2 oz Baileys Salted Caramel
- 1.25 oz Heavy Cream
- Shake with ice
- Drizzle Caramel in glass
- Fill with ice
