Let’s start 2023 with class and refinement by shaking up a “Dapper Beagle.” 🍸Made for the fantastic [@Lycaos_Wolf](https://twitter.com/Lycaos_Wolf) ❤️ Big Wolfy thanks for all the kindness, love, and support Awoo!

## Ingredients

- 3 oz Bourbon
- 1 oz Fire & Spiced Cherry Syrup
- 0.5 oz Lemon Juice
- 3 Dashes Orange Bitters
- Shake with ice
- Slice orange, Dust with sugar, and torch
- Serve over ice with 3 Spiced Maraschino Cherries
