## Ingredients

- 2 scoops of mint chocolate chip ice cream
- 4 oz whole milk
- 3.5 oz Bailey’s Irish Cream
- Mix in a blender
- Add a drizzle of chocolate in a glass
- Pour
- Top with fresh whipped cream and a shamrock cookie
