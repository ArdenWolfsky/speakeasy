Looking for a good time? Need to unwind? 😈 Join us in the darkness and lose yourself in a sea of Neon, a “Neon Night.” Made for the adorable and incredible [@Neon_woof](https://twitter.com/Neon_woof) 🐺🍸Cheers Awoo!

## Ingredients

- 1 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Gin
- 1 oz Malibu Rum
- 1 oz Tequila
- 1 oz Blue Curaçao
- 1 oz Lemon Juice
- 1 Scoop of Pineapple Sorbet
- Shake with ice
- Pour 1 oz Blue Curaçao into a glass
- Fill with ice
- Slowly pour over ice
- Top with fresh pineapple & cherry
