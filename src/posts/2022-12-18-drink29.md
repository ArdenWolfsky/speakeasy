## Rosemary Syrup

- 0.5 Cup water
- 0.5 Cup sugar
- 3 Rosemary sprigs
- Boil together and let cool

## Ingredients

- 3 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Rosemary Syrup
- 0.5 oz Lemon Juice
- Shake with ice Top with Ginger beer
- Garnish with Rosemary and Cranberries
