## Jellybean Vodka

- 30 Fruity Jellybeans (Starburst is best)
- 10 oz Vodka
- 0.25 tsp Pink Luster dust
- Mix in a jar and let sit overnight

## Ingredients

- 3 oz Jellybean Vodka
- 2 oz Orange Liqueur
- 1 oz lemon juice
- Shake with ice
