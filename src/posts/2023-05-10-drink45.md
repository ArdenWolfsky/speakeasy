## Ingredients

- 4 oz Pineapple juice
- 3 oz Gin
- 0.5 oz Lemon juice
- Shake with ice
- 2 oz Raspberry Lambic
- Fill glass with ice
- Pour mix over ice
- Garnish with fresh lime and mint
