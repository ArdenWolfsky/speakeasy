## Ingredients

- 4 oz Fresh or frozen mango
- 2 oz Spiced Dark Rum
- 1 oz Orange juice
- 1.5 oz Orange liqueur
- 1 oz Maple syrup
- Mix in a blender
- Top with Kinky Pink Liqueur
