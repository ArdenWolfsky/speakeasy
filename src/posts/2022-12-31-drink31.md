## Grape Syrup

- 1 cup Red grapes
- 0.5 cup Sugar
- 0.5 cup Water
- 1 tbsp Lemon juice
- Bring to a boil, simmer for 10 mins, then cool

## Ingredients

- 1 oz Grape Simple Syrup
- 0.5 oz Cognac
- 0.25 oz Lemon Juice
- Shake with Ice
- 1/8 tsp Gold Luster Dust
- Top with Champagne
