## Base Mix

- 0.5 oz Curaçao
- 1 oz Sour Mix
- 2 oz Cranberry Juice
- 2 oz Vodka
- 1.5 oz Simple Syrup
- 0.25 tsp Sliver Lust (Optional)

## Blue Mix

- 0.5 oz Curaçao
- 0.5 Vodka
- 0.25 tsp Blue Lust (Optional)
- Shake with ice

## Foam Top

- 1 oz Pasteurized Egg Whites
- 2 oz Triple Sec
- Dry shake for 20 seconds
