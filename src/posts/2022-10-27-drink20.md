Let’s give chase to the weekend and enjoy the hunt with “A Blue Foxtail! Made for the adorable [@JayKubJK](https://twitter.com/JayKubJK) 🦊Awoo! ❤️🐺🍸

## Ingredients

- 2 oz Blue Curaçao
- 1.5 oz 99 Blackberries
- Shake with ice
- 0.25 tsp Silver Luster Dust
- 2 oz Raspberry Vodka
- Mix and fill the glass with ice
- Top with shaken mix
- Splashes of Heavy Cream
- Spin or stir lightly
