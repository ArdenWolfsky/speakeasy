## Ingredients

- 2.5 oz Lemon Rum
- 1 oz Triple Sec
- 1 oz Cranberry juice
- 1 oz Egg white squeeze of lemon
- Dry shake
- Add ice & Shake again
- 1 oz Grenadine
- Garnish with gummy Hearts
