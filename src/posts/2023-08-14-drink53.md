## Ingredients

- 1.5 oz Marshmallow Vodka
- 1 oz Irish cream
- 1 oz Heavy cream
- 1 oz Chocolate syrup
- 0.5 oz Drambuie
- Shake with ice
- Top with toasted marshmallows
