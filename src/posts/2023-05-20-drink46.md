It's happy hour fluffs! So shake of those Grumpy weekday vibes and join the good times with the [@TopDogsPodcast](https://twitter.com/TopDogsPodcast) crew and enjoy a “Fictitiously Grumpy” cocktail tonight! 🍸

## Base Mix

- 2 oz Gin
- 1 oz Crème de Violette
- 1 oz Lemon Juice
- 0.5 oz Maraschino Liqueur
- Shake with ice
- Fill glass with ice and pour

## Shaker 2

- 1 oz Lemon Juice
- 0.5 oz Blue Curaçao
- 1 oz Gin
- 1 oz Egg white
- Dry Shake
- Pour over base mixture
