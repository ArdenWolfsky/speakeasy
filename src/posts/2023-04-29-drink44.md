## Ingredients

- 2 oz Peanut butter Whisky
- 2 oz Milk
- 4 Reese’s cups
- 2 tbsp Smooth peanut butter
- 2 Scoops vanilla Ice Cream
- Mix in a blender
- Top with whip cream and Reese’s cups pieces
