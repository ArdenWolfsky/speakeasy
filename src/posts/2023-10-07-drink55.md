## Ingredients

- 1 Apple Sliced
- 0.5 Cup Brown Sugar
- 2 Cinnamon Sticks
- 0.5 Cup Fireball Whisky
- Cook for 10 minutes, then cool

## Pitcher

In a pitcher with ice, mix:

- 1 Bottle of White Wine
- 2 Cups Apple Cider
- 1 Cup Maple Whisky
- 1 Orange Sliced
- Cooked Apple mix
