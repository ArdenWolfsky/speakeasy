## Ingredients

- 2 oz Malibu Coconut Rum
- 1 oz Spiced Rum
- 0.5 oz Gin
- 0.25 oz Razzmatazz
- 0.25 oz Grenadine
- 2 oz Orange Juice
- 2 oz Pineapple Juice
- Combine all ingredients and shake with ice.
- Fill glass with ice
- Pour
- Top with 0.5 oz Grenadine
