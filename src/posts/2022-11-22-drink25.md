## Shaker 1

- 1 oz Orange juice
- 1 oz Sour Mix
- 0.5 oz Pineapple juice
- 2.5 oz Orange Vodka
- 1 oz Egg whites
- 0.25 tsp Black Food Gel

## Shaker 2

- 0.5 Orange Vodka
- 1.5 oz Simple Syrup
- 1/8 tsp Sliver Food Lust
- Serve over ice
