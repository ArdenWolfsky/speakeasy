Monday can be a lovely day when you have a wonderful “Zetty Zet” with you! A bright and rich custom cocktail for the marvelous [@ZettiosDorne](https://twitter.com/ZettiosDorne) Cheers Awoo! 🍸❤️

## Ingredients

- 2.5 oz Vanilla Vodka
- 3 oz Amaretto Liqueur
- 1 oz Crème de Cacao
- Shake with ice
- Serve over ice
- Top with 0.5 oz Caramel Creamer
