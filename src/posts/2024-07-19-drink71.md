## Ingredients

- 1 Wine glass of Gin
- 0.75 tablespoon of sugar
- 3 or 4 dashes of lime or lemon juice
- 1 bottle of plain soda water
- Stir & serve over ice with lemon and a cherry

## Non-Alcoholic

- 1 Wine glass of Rituals Gin Alternative
- 0.75 tablespoon of sugar
- 3 or 4 dashes of lime or lemon juice
- 1 bottle of plain soda water
- Stir & serve over ice with lemon and a cherry
