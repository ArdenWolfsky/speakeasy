## Ingredients

- 2 oz Vodka
- 2 oz Malibu Rum
- 1 oz Sweet & Sour mix
- 2 oz Lemonade
- Splash of Blue Curaçao
- Shake with ice
- Place jellybeans & fish gummies in glass
- Pour & top with sprite
- Garnish with fresh lemon & umbrella.
