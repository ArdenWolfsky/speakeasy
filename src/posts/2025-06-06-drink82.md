## Fruits

- 3 Cups Red Grapes
- 3 Cups Blueberries
- 3 Cups Kiwi
- 3 Cups Pineapple
- 3 Cups Cantaloupe
- 3 Cups Strawberries

## Make

- 4 oz Bourbon
- 4 oz Triple Sec
- 2 oz Agave
- 2 Bottles Stella Rosa Honey Peach Sparkling Wine
- Pour over fruit and serve!
