## Ingredients

- 8 Blackberries
- 1 oz Lemon Juice
- 2 oz Vodka
- 2 oz Chambord
- 1 oz Maple Syrup

## Make

- Add blackberries to the shaker and muddle until smooth.
- Combine the remaining ingredients in the shaker and add ice.
- Shake for 5-10 seconds.
- Serve in a coupe glass.
- Add a sprig of fresh rosemary and sear with a torch until smoky.
